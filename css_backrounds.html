The Nintendo DSi

The Nintendo DSi was a handheld console first released by Nintendo in November 2008. It had bigger screens compared to the DS and had a front and back camera with a microphone included. There was also a bigger version called the DSi XL.

The DSi came in a lot of colors and had two screens. It could play DS games only unlike its predicessors (DS and Ds lite,that could also play GBA games) It sold 101 million units. The DSi also had a SD Card slot for expanded storage.

The DSi had a Stylus,Cameras Mic and a touch screen
