The new Macbook Neo
The Macbook neo is Apples cheapest Macbook (Starting at 699€ or 599€ with student discount.) It has the same aluminium and 13'' liqid retna display as the Mac Air and Pro. It has the A18 Pro chip inside. It has two USB Ports. It comes in four colors: Blue, Pink, Green and Silver
The MacBook Neo comes in two models: 256GB storage and no touch id and 512GB storage with touch id.
