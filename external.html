PS Vita

The PS Vita was the succesor to the PSP (Playstation Portable). It had a front and back camera, a touchpad at the back and a Gyro Sensor,along wth two analog sticks and an Oled screen on the normal model(It had a LCD screen on the lite.)
The PS Vita nowadays is usually modded and can run a lot of emulators when modded.
The console came in three models: The oringinal (PS Vita 1000), The 3G version (PS Vita 1004) and the PS Vita lite. All of them had the same specs(Execpt the PS Vita 2000 with the different screen, smaller touchpads and plasic instead of metal.)

The 3G model is same to the PS Vita 1000, but it had a 3G modem and was made only in Japan.
