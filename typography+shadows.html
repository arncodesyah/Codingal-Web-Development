Gameboy Advance


The Gameboy Advance was the third generation of nintendo handheld consoles. There were three versions of the GBA

  • Gameboy Advance
  • Gameboy Advance SP
  • Gameboy Advance Micro

The Gameboy Advance could play Gameboy games too and also was smaller than the oringinal gameboy, but had a dim screen.

The Gameboy Advance SP was foldable and had a lit screen and could also play gameboy games.

The Micro was released in 2005 and had interchangeble faceplates but could not play gameboy games unlike the GBA and GBA SP.
