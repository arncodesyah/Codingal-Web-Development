About us

Kryptic Space and Areonautics(KSAA) is a private aerospace company focused on advancing spaceflight through reusable rockets, innovative spacecraft, and orbital infrastructure. Our flagship project, KerbolLink, is a satellite internet network designed to connect Kerbin, spacecraft, and future colonies across the Kerbol System. Driven by rapid innovation and ambitious exploration, KSAA is building the technology that will make a connected, spacefaring future possible.

Our Projects

Kerbollink

KSAA's satellite communications constellation, providing reliable coverage and connectivity across Kerbin and supporting missions throughout the Kerbol System. KerbolLink is the backbone of KSAA's future space infrastructure.

Rockets

KSAA develops reusable and high-performance rockets designed for satellite deployment, crew transport, cargo delivery, and deep-space exploration. Our launch systems are built to make access to space more efficient and affordable.

Bases

KSAA operates a growing network of launch sites, tracking stations, research centers, and space habitats. These facilities support mission operations, spacecraft development, and the expansion of Kerbal presence beyond Kerbin.
